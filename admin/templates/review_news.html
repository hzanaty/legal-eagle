💡 How to Use This Page

Relevance Scoring Guide

  • 1 - Not Relevant: Completely unrelated to the profile's LoB (HCM/FIN). Can be safely ignored.
  • 2 - Low: Tangentially related but not actionable. General news that happens to mention a keyword.
  • 3 - Medium: Somewhat relevant. Could be useful context but not a direct legal change.
  • 4 - High: Relevant to the LoB. Consider adding keywords to the glossary.
  • 5 - Very Relevant: This should have been included! Add missing terms to the glossary immediately.

Status Badges

  • ✅ Accepted: Item passed all filters and was accepted into baseline. Review to confirm AI didn't let through irrelevant content.
  • ⚠️ Filtered: Item was filtered out. Review to check if AI was too strict and missed relevant content.

Filter Reasons

  • ✓ Passed: Item passed all filters (accepted items).
  • Domain/Country: The news source domain wasn't whitelisted and no country mention was found.
  • Glossary: No glossary terms matched in the content. Consider adding relevant terms.
  • LLM: The AI classified this as not relevant to the LoB. Review the content to verify.
💡 Tip: High scores (4-5) on filtered items indicate missing glossary terms. Low scores (1-2) on accepted items indicate AI is letting through irrelevant content. Update the glossary to improve future filtering.
