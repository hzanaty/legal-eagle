# Project Brief

## Core Requirements and Goals
The Legal Eagle project aims to build an AI-driven agent for continuously monitoring government sources for legal updates. Focused on specific countries and industries like HR & Payroll (HCM) or Finance (FIN), it assesses impacts on SAP’s localized products and delivers actionable intelligence to stakeholders.

## Fundamental Goals
1. Develop a centralized system for detecting and processing legal changes.
2. Minimize latency in identifying changes affecting SAP localized solutions.
3. Provide actionable insights for Localization Product Managers to influence product strategies.
