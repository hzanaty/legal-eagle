Legal Change Summary — Decision 32689 / ΦΕΚ Β' 6745 / 16.12.2025

Ministerial Decision 32689/12.12.2025 amends Decision 40331/Δ1.13521/13-9-2019. It introduces a new framework of digital declarations for work time organization, employment changes, hiring, termination, collective agreements, and work card reporting under ERGANI II. Effective 16 February 2026.

The decision covers Articles 2.1, 2.3, 2.4 (incl. sub-sections 2A-A through 2A-E), 2.6, 2.7, 2.8, 2.10, and 2.11 — each mapped to a specific XML schema replacing or superseding prior ERGANI I reporting mechanisms.

⚠️ All legacy E-forms (E1–E12) are abolished by this decision. No dual submission. ERGANI II digital declarations are the sole reporting channel as of 16.02.2026. Customers no longer submit E3, E4, E5, E6, E7, E10, E11, or any other E-series form.

Mapping 1 — How f_type is derived

For every absence record, the program looks up T7GR1HR19 using IT2001 AWART. If found: f_type = EABCD (full-day) or EABCH (hourly). If not found: absence is silently skipped in XML.

Mapping 2 — How F_year and F_req_days are derived

Only triggered when IT2001 SUBTY ∈ s_abssub. Implemented in method modify_absence_shifts of lcl_hgrle2v0_work_schedule.
StepObjectKey ConditionOutput
1 IT2001 P2001 PERNR + BEGDA/ENDDA = absence date
SUBTY ∈ s_abssub		 DOCNR
2 PTQUODED DOCNR + DATUM + PERNR QUONR
3 PA2006 PERNR + SUBTY ∈ s_abqsub + QUONR BEGDA → F_year = BEGDA[1:4]
4 IT2006 P2006 PERNR + SUBTY ∈ s_abqsub
Full quota year · BEGDA ≠ ENDDA		 SUM ANZHL → F_req_days
If any step yields no result: F_year and F_req_days are written as empty elements — no error raised.
