## Grounding Notes

- **5746** and **4691** are core Turkey HCM/payroll references because R&D and technology-zone incentives can affect payroll taxation and social-security treatment.
- **1475** should remain in the reference set because Article 14 continues to be relevant for severance-pay rules even though the broader law was superseded.
- **4447** is important for unemployment-insurance and employment-support measures, including temporary incentive/support provisions.
- Keep **Authority** to one canonical authority per row to avoid ambiguous AI grounding.
- Gazette numbers, Presidential Decision numbers, Circular numbers, and other publication references should be captured separately at news/reference level rather than in the law number field.
