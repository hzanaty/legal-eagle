# Legal Eagle Strategic Roadmap v4

## From Legal Eagle Core to Virtual Localization Product Manager

---

# Current Status — Ready Now

## Legal Eagle Core ✅

The foundational Legal Eagle platform is already operational today.

### Existing Capabilities

* Monitoring legal and regulatory sources
* Collecting updates from RSS, websites, social media, and official sources
* AI-based relevance filtering
* Deduplication and change comparison
* Grouping and summarizing news
* Searchable historical intelligence baseline
* Daily digest generation
* Multi-country / multi-profile support
* Admin and configuration framework

### Current Strategic Position

Legal Eagle Core already acts as a **Regulatory Intelligence Engine** and forms the base for all future expansion.

---

# Vision

Transform Legal Eagle Core into a reusable **Localization Intelligence Platform** that evolves in stages:

**Journalist → Lawyer → SAP Consultant → Virtual LPM → Executive Advisor**

Each stage is enabled by dedicated MCP / Skills capabilities.

---

# Evolution Journey with MCP Roles

---

## Stage 1 — Journalist

## Primary MCP: Legal Eagle MCP

Legal Eagle becomes externally consumable through MCP / Skills / APIs.

### Role

Trusted source of fresh regulatory intelligence.

### Example Capabilities

* latest_news()
* daily_digest()
* search_news()
* list_profiles()

### Purpose

Detect what changed externally.

### User Model

LPM manually combines Legal Eagle with other MCPs.

---

## Stage 2 — Lawyer

## Primary MCP: Legal Analyzer MCP

Build the legal reasoning layer.

### Role

Interpret law changes like a legal expert.

### Example Capabilities

* analyze_article()
* explain_change()
* compare_old_new_article()
* linked_articles()
* summarize_obligations()

### Purpose

Understand what the law actually means.

### Notes

Initially internal component, later exposed as standalone MCP.

---

## Stage 3 — SAP Consultant

## Primary MCPs: Taxonomy MCP + Legal Analyzer MCP

Convert legal meaning into SAP solution impact.

### Role

Translate legal change into implementable SAP requirements.

### Example Capabilities

* impacted_modules()
* business_term_to_sap_object()
* process_mapping()
* draft_prd_outline()
* config_vs_enhancement_guidance()

### Purpose

Answer:

* What SAP products are affected?
* Which objects/processes are impacted?
* Standard solution or enhancement?

### Notes

Taxonomy MCP provides mapping power.
Legal Analyzer enriches legal context.

---

## Stage 4 — Smart Legal Eagle MCP

## Primary MCPs: Legal Eagle MCP + Legal Analyzer MCP + Taxonomy MCP

Upgrade Legal Eagle provider services into smarter capabilities.

### Example Capabilities

* solution_impact_summary()
* analyze_latest_change()
* country_change_brief()
* legal_to_solution_summary()

### Purpose

One request gives news + meaning + SAP impact.

---

## Stage 5 — Virtual LPM (Orchestrator)

## Primary MCPs: Legal Eagle MCP + Legal Analyzer MCP + Taxonomy MCP + LOKA MCP

Full orchestration layer.

### Role

Behaves like a Localization Product Manager assistant.

### Intelligent Flow

1. Legal Eagle MCP detects change
2. Legal Analyzer MCP explains law
3. Taxonomy MCP maps SAP impact
4. LOKA MCP checks precedents, JIRA, RCM, docs
5. Return structured recommendation

### Example Capabilities

* full_lpm_brief()
* similar_change_history()
* recommended_next_actions()
* draft_requirement_summary()

---

## Stage 6 — Executive Advisor

## Primary MCPs: All MCPs + Future Planning Services

Management decision support layer.

### Role

Support leadership and portfolio planning.

### Example Capabilities

* priority_dashboard()
* cross_country_risk_map()
* capacity_vs_legal_demand()
* roadmap_pressure_report()
* readiness_overview()

Provides reusable capabilities to LPMs:

* News
* Digests
* Search
* Legal analysis
* Solution summaries

## Legal Eagle as Consumer

Consumes enterprise MCPs:

* Legal Analyzer MCP
* Taxonomy MCP
* LOKA MCP
* Future Skills / APIs

* Faster delivery
* Lower risk
* Future-proof design
* Easy integration path
