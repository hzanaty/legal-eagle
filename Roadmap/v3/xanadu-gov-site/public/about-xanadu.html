Our nation

From independence to a modern public service

Xanadu is a young sovereign nation founded on dignity at work, shared prosperity and public institutions that earn trust through clear service.

Independence

After generations of regional administration, Xanadu declared peaceful independence on 17 January 2025. The Founding Charter committed the new Republic to representative institutions, equal citizenship, social protection and an economy open to responsible investment.

The selection of Queen Olivia

During the Constitutional Assembly, civic councils from every province selected Olivia as Xanadu's first constitutional Queen and guardian of the Founding Charter. Her role is to protect institutional continuity, convene the Council of State and represent the Republic at home and abroad.

Building the first authorities

The new government placed workforce stability among its earliest priorities. Employers needed common rules, workers needed dependable protection, and the country needed skills for rapid development. The Ministry of Human Resources was therefore established as one of Xanadu's first national authorities.

The founding promise

Modernization in Xanadu must strengthen both enterprise and dignity at work: simple rules, fair protections and enough notice for employers to comply.

Founding timeline

17 January 2025 — Independence

The Founding Charter establishes the sovereign Republic of Xanadu.

2 February 2025 — Queen selected

The Constitutional Assembly selects Olivia as constitutional Queen.

10 February 2025 — First authorities formed

The Council of State creates the first ministries, including Human Resources.

1 May 2025 — Employment framework begins

The Ministry opens employer services and starts consultation on national labor standards.
