One week remaining before the new social insurance contribution rate takes effect

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources · Reference MoHR/SI/2026/114

The Ministry of Human Resources reminds all registered employers that the new social insurance contribution rate takes effect on 12 September 2026, one week from today. Employers should complete their preparations without delay.

From the first payroll cycle on or after the effective date, the revised social insurance contribution rate must be applied to all covered employees. Payroll and HR systems that still use the previous rate must be updated before that cycle is processed.

This notice follows the Ministry's earlier announcements setting out the reform of the social insurance contribution rate. The rate itself and the scope of covered employees remain as previously communicated; today's notice concerns only the approaching effective date and the steps employers must take in the coming week.

Employers who process payroll through an external provider are advised to confirm that the provider will apply the new rate on time. Questions may be directed to the Ministry's employer support desk.
