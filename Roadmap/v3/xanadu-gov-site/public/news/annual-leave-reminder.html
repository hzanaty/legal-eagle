Reminder: submit outstanding annual leave requests before year-end

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources

Staff are reminded to file any remaining annual leave requests through the internal portal before 31 December.

Requests submitted after the deadline may not be carried over, in line with the Ministry's standing internal guidance. Line managers are asked to review pending requests promptly.

This is an internal administrative reminder and does not change any leave entitlement.
