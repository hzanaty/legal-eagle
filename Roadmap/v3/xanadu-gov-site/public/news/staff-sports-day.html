Ministry holds annual staff sports day at headquarters

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources

Employees from across departments took part in the Ministry's annual staff sports day, held at the headquarters grounds.

The event promotes wellbeing and team spirit among staff. Activities included friendly team competitions and a group walk.

The Ministry thanks the organising committee and all participants.
