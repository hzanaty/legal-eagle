Regional HR Bureau relocates to new premises in Central District

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources

The Central District regional bureau of the Ministry will operate from a new address effective next month. The move is part of a routine facilities upgrade.

Public counter services will continue without interruption during and after the relocation. The new premises offer improved accessibility and additional waiting capacity.

Visitors are advised to note the updated address before their next visit.
