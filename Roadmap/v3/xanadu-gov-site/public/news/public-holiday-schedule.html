Ministry publishes the 2027 public holiday schedule

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources

The Ministry of Human Resources has published the official list of national public holidays for the calendar year 2027 for public reference.

The schedule is provided for planning purposes. Where a public holiday falls on a weekend, the customary substitute-day arrangements apply as in previous years.

The full list is available at Ministry service counters and through the public information portal.
